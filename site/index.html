Fleet leasing & rental ERP

Role
Full-stack developer, release owner
Period
Feb 2022 – present
Compliance
ZATCA (Saudi e-invoicing)

The problem

Leasing and rental businesses need contracts, invoicing and collections that stay compliant with Saudi e-invoicing rules and post cleanly into their ERP, across several client deployments.

What I built

  • Billing core: invoicing events, contracts, collections and refunds across every payment mode, cash registers, bank deposits, settlements and accruals.
  • ZATCA e-invoicing library: UBL XML generation, cryptographic signing, QR codes, BR-KSA validation and POS invoice numbering.
  • Epicor ERP integration: auto-posting of invoices, collections and refunds, fiscal-period handling, Kafka consumers and retry jobs.
  • Shared platform modules: approval workflows, idempotency and round-robin assignment, used across the service estate.
  • Releases: I own versioning, pipelines and multi-client deployments on Kubernetes (GKE), plus production data fixes.

Corporate vehicle-sharing platform

Role

Period

Scope
Nearly every microservice

The problem

Companies sharing a vehicle pool need self-service booking, keyless access and clear accountability for every trip, damage and parking event.

What I built

  • Booking & contract lifecycle: advance bookings, overlap checks, force-close, no-show and auto-cancel, cost calculation.
  • Admin control panel (Next.js) and API gateway: the codebases I've contributed to most.
  • Telematics: vehicle commands (lock/unlock), geofences and parking polygons, automatic out-of-parking status.
  • Accountability: damage checklists with email and push notifications, activity logs and contract flow history.
  • Multi-tenant platform: roles and permissions, announcements and Cube.js reporting.
